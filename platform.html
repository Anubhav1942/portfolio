Case studies and work

How it works

Up and live in minutes

1

Create an account

Sign up with your email. You'll get a verification link, click it, and you're in. No waitlist, no approval process.

2

Fill in your details

Your admin panel is waiting. Add your name, role, bio, work experience, and case studies. Everything saves automatically as you type.

3

Connect your domain

Buy a domain from any registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare -- doesn't matter). Point it to GitHub Pages with a CNAME, then register it in your settings panel. Done.

4

Share it

yourname.com is now live, pulling your content from the cloud, and updating whenever you make a change in the admin panel.
